        Career Growth

        The Engineering Ladder at LOGIX

        We provide an explicit, merit-based career path for every engineer.

        Level 01

        Software Engineer

        Focuses on shipping high-quality components, implementing typed REST/GraphQL APIs, and writing automated unit and E2E tests.

        Level 02

        Senior Engineer

        Owns sub-system architecture, leads complex database schema migrations, conducts rigorous peer code reviews, and mentors juniors.

        Level 03

        Staff / Lead Architect

        Designs multi-cloud infrastructure, drafts company-wide RFC standards, solves difficult distributed systems bottlenecks, and interfaces with client leadership.

        Level 04

        Principal Fellow

        Sets the overarching technology strategy, leads frontier AI model integration, and shapes the long-term technological vision of LOGIX.
